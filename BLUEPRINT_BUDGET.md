## Notes

- The LCSC carts include MOQ overhead for small parts like resistors, capacitors, and diodes. That is why the cart total is higher than a single-board per-part estimate.
- The Front HMI LCSC screenshot shows one backordered item, so I am leaving a little price/stock buffer in the submission notes instead of pretending the number is frozen forever.
- The JLCPCB screenshot has one older HMI board still visible but not selected. The selected PCBs are the Main Shield ($7.00) and the logo/sign HMI ($4.10), for $11.10 merchandise total.
- No PCBA service is being used. The board will be hand-soldered.
